Practice data

Ready-made raw data for learning lookups and analysis. Click Copy data, click cell A1 of a blank sheet, and press Ctrl + V — the data lands in neat columns. Or download the CSV and open it in Excel.

How to use. Paste each table into its own sheet in one workbook. Keep the first table on Sheet1 and paste lookup tables onto Sheet2 — that is exactly how real office lookup workbooks are organised. Every dataset below is structured so you can practise VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX + MATCH, IFERROR and approximate-match lookups immediately.

Feeling stuck? The full course walks through every lookup step by step — start with Level 2: Formulas and Functions, then continue to Level 3: Data Management. The formula reference has quick syntax for all of them.
